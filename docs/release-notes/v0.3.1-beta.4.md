# Pelmet 0.3.1-beta.4

Fourth beta of 0.3.1, on the beta channel only (Settings › About › "Get beta releases"). Lighter at idle, and the clock click behaves.

Fixed:

- A click on the clock no longer moves your pointer (#74, thanks
  @brodly). Pelmet replays that click because macOS refuses it while
  anything is hidden, and the replay dragged the pointer to where the
  click had landed. Pelmet presses the clock directly now, and the one
  replay left lands under your pointer where it is.
- The flash on a clock click is quick again. The picture Pelmet keeps
  of the empty bar was retaken on every click after a reveal, 250ms
  each; it is kept until an icon of Pelmet's own really changes.
- With a smooth reveal on a second display, the Sound icon slid with
  the others instead of popping in once they had settled.
- A quick hover in and out no longer shows the hidden icons twice, one
  set standing still while the other slid out.
- Pelmet uses less CPU while you move the mouse. It used to look at
  every pointer move on the main thread; now a listener thread passes
  on only the moves near the menu bar.

Also:

- The menu bar's ink (light or dark) is broadcast to the apps Pelmet
  hides, so an app drawing its own status band can match it.
